Your Step-by-Step Printing Guide

Simple, fast, and reliable. Follow these steps to print your school documents with ease:

Go to the “Submit” page on the PrintDesk website.

Choose the document you want to print from your device and upload the file

Customize your print options such as choosing paper size, number of copies, color or grayscale and layout

Review your submission by checking the number of tokens that will be consumed and what place you are in the queue.

Wait for your print job to be accepted and once printed claim at the designated printing station

Semester-Based Reset

Each student receives 500 tokens every semester, used to pay for print jobs.

Carry-Over Policy

Unused tokens will not carry over to the next semester and will be reset at the start of each new semester.

Fair Use Policies

PRINT VALUES

STANDARD DOCUMENTS

Black and White documents containing text and minimal images are priced at 1 token per page, provided that images occupy less than 60% of the page. Can be of any size.

Colored documents containing text and minimal images are priced at 10 tokens per page, provided that images occupy less than 60% of the page. Can be of any size.

IMAGE PRINTS

Black and White documents containing mostly images are priced at 10 tokens per page, provided that images occupy greater than 60% of the page. Can be of any size.

Colored documents containing mostly images are priced at 15 tokens per page, provided that images occupy greater than 60% of the page. Can be of any size.
